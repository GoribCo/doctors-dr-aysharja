## What concerns can I discuss at an orthopedic consultation?

You can discuss bone and joint pain, injuries, stiffness, back or neck problems, and difficulties with movement. See the [services page](/services/) for the areas of care listed on this site.

## What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring previous prescriptions, relevant X-rays or scan reports, and a list of medicines you currently take. Note when your symptoms started and how they affect daily activities.

## How do I arrange an appointment?

Visit the [appointment page](/appointment/) for booking information, or use the [contact page](/contact/) to reach the clinic.

## Can I discuss recovery after an injury or operation?

Yes. Bring your discharge summary and previous treatment records so that recovery progress and follow-up needs can be discussed.
